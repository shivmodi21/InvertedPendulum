02 / MATHEMATICAL MODEL

From physics to feedback

01

Nonlinear dynamics

The cart and pole motion are integrated from the nonlinear equations of motion using SciPy's adaptive RK45 solver.

x = [p, ṗ, θ, θ̇]ᵀ
02

Linearization

A state-space model around the upright equilibrium provides the local approximation used to design the feedback controller.

ẋ = Ax + Bu
03

Hybrid controller

An energy-shaping swing-up controller injects energy after the natural fall. When the pole enters the capture region, LQR feedback stabilizes it upright.

Swing-up → LQR
